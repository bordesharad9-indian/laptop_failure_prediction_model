About This Project (Easy Overview)

How Our Laptop Hardware Failure Prediction System Works

Smart Hardware Health Monitor
1. Reads 9 Laptop Sensors Step 1

Collects live values from your laptop: CPU & GPU Temperature, CPU & GPU Usage, RAM Usage, Disk Space, Disk Speed (I/O), Battery Level, and Fan Speed.

2. Checks Safe vs. Danger Limits Step 2

Compares every sensor reading with normal safe limits so the system can immediately spot overheating, slow cooling fans, full memory, or low battery stress.

3. Predicts Failure Risk (%) Step 3

Uses our trained Machine Learning model (Random Forest & Gradient Boosting) to calculate the exact Failure Risk % and show if the laptop is Healthy or at Risk of Failure.

4. Gives Simple Safety Advice Step 4

Tells you the exact problem and how to fix it early—such as cleaning the cooling fan, closing heavy apps, freeing disk space, or plugging in the charger.
